Support

This page collects support information for TitechApp. We recommend checking the FAQ first before contacting us.

Contact us

For questions, bug reports or feature requests, please use the Google Form below. You can also open the same form from the "More" tab in the app ("Contact us"), which automatically fills in your device model, OS version and app version.

Contact form (Google Forms)

When reporting a problem, please include the following information if possible:

  • Device model (e.g. iPhone 17 Pro, MacBook Air M3)
  • OS version (e.g. iOS 19.2, macOS 16.0)
  • TitechApp version (shown at the bottom of the "More" tab)
  • Steps to reproduce the issue
  • Screenshots of the error or the relevant screen
